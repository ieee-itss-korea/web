Here are the presentation videos of the six winners of the **first Three Minute Thesis (3MT) competition** held by the IEEE ITSS Korea Chapter in August 2026. All six winners agreed to share their videos. Select a title to watch the video on YouTube.

- **Grand Prize**: Gabriel Manalu, KAIST, [Evaluating Vision-Language Models (VLMs) for Corner-Case Understanding in Autonomous Driving](https://youtu.be/ZaaV40dTlzU)
- **First Prize**: Sangeun Park, Sungkyunkwan University, [Interaction-Aware Autonomous Driving in Mixed Traffic](https://youtu.be/dRKRVnQvfvk)
- **Second Prize**: Hae Chang Kim, Yonsei University, [Reliable velocity estimation by radar with a memory in heavy traffic](https://youtu.be/nrjZ3IV0TGA)
- **Encouragement Prize**: Seonmyeong Lee, KAIST, [Continual Learning in Autonomous Driving Perception](https://youtu.be/o_4xWaTmne0)
- **Encouragement Prize**: Bon Jae Choe, KAIST, [Park Dense, Leave Free](https://youtu.be/7gv3FqX6Yls)
- **Encouragement Prize**: Eun-Ji Lee, Korea National University of Transportation, [Validation of Virtual Driving Data Using a Digital Twin Simulation Based on NVIDIA Omniverse](https://youtu.be/jIQhjnpHHC0)

The Chapter congratulates all six winners once again.
