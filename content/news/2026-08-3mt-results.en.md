In August 2026 the **IEEE ITSS Korea Chapter held its first Three Minute Thesis (3MT) competition** — a format in which students and early-career researchers present their research to a general audience in just three minutes.

**Six prizes** were presented to the top entries, and **participation certificates** to every entrant. The competition gave the Chapter's student and early-career members a stage to distill and communicate their work — a skill that carries into every conference presentation and paper.

**Maryam Kaveshgar, IEEE ITSS Vice President for Member Activities, sent a congratulatory video** for the ceremony — a warm recognition of the Chapter's first 3MT from the Society's leadership.

The Chapter congratulates all participants and looks forward to making 3MT a recurring opportunity for its members.
