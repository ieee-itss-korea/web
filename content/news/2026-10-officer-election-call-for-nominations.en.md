**The call for nominations for the IEEE ITSS Korea Chapter's first formal officer election (2027-2028 terms) was sent by the Election Committee from IEEE vTools to Chapter members by email on October 19, 2026.** The subject line is "Korea Council Chapter, ITSS38 Call for Nominations"; if you cannot find it, please check your spam folder. If you did not receive it at all, please tell us through the [contact form](https://ieee-itss-korea.github.io/web/en/contact/).

## Schedule

All times are Korea time.

| Step | Date |
|------|------|
| Call for nominations sent | Monday, October 19, 2026 |
| Nominations close | Monday, November 9, 2026, 23:59 |
| Petitions close | Saturday, November 14, 2026, 23:59 (the petition opening will be announced to members) |
| Voting starts | Monday, November 30, 2026, by vTools online ballot (the Election Committee's schedule) |
| Election completed | By December 15 (IEEE rules) |

## How to Nominate

- **Positions:** Chair, Vice Chair, Secretary, Treasurer (two-year terms starting January 1, 2027)
- **Who can be nominated:** yourself or another member who was a member of this Chapter on March 31, 2026 and is of a grade eligible to serve.
- **How:** use [the vTools nomination page](https://nominations.vtools.ieee.org/tego_/nominate_or_petition/CH11217) and sign in with your IEEE account. Each candidate presents a biography and a statement of plan.

## Election Committee

The Election Committee was formed after the Chapter ExCom raised no objection. Its members are not current officers and may not stand in this election.

- Chair: Prof. Shiho Kim (Yonsei University)
- Members: Prof. Seok-Cheol Kee (Chungbuk National University), Prof. Seung-Woo Seo (Seoul National University)

## Contact

Questions about the election can be sent through the [contact form](https://ieee-itss-korea.github.io/web/en/contact/). We will share progress on this page and in the Chapter's channels.
