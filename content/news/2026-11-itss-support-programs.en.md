## Support for chapter activities

IEEE ITSS supports chapter activities, Distinguished Lecturer visits and student chapters. The [ITSS Request for Funding page](https://ieee-itss.org/chapters/funding/) lists a maximum of US$2,000 (student branches: up to US$500).

If you have an idea for a workshop, talk or hands-on session you would like to hold with our Chapter in 2027, please tell us through the [contact form](https://forms.gle/zFyReJdxHULLFrEY8).

## 2027 New Initiatives: call for proposals

IEEE ITSS has issued its call for 2027 New Initiatives proposals. Only IEEE ITSS members may submit, and in principle each proposal's total budget may not exceed US$40,000. The deadline is 11:59 PM US Pacific Time on Sunday 31 January 2027, which is 4:59 PM KST on Monday 1 February.

See the [ITSS LinkedIn post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/itssieee_fund-itss-intelligent-activity-7531075649919438849-ZR9z) for the conditions and how to submit.
