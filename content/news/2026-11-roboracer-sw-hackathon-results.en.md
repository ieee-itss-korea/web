---

In this online hackathon, co-hosted by the IEEE ITSS Korea Chapter and Chungbuk National University, 25 teams submitted code before the deadline and were auto-graded. Their 53 members received participation certificates.

From 16 to 23 November, teams wrote automatic emergency braking (AEB) and Follow the Gap software for an F1TENTH autonomous race car. Teams that met the requirements for both were ranked by simulation lap time.

The ceremony was held at Chungbuk National University on Wednesday 25 November. Congratulations to the top three teams, and thank you to all participants and to our co-host. Special thanks to Prof. Kee of Chungbuk National University for helping from the first format discussion through the ceremony.
