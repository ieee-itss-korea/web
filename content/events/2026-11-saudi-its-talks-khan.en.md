## Abstract

The talk explores how Quantum Intelligence can complement AI in next-generation intelligent transportation systems, including traffic optimization, mobility prediction, V2X communication, intelligent routing, and real-time decision-making. It also highlights research opportunities and challenges related to hardware limitations, scalability, algorithm maturity, and integration with existing transportation infrastructure.

## Topics Covered

- How Quantum Intelligence can complement AI in next-generation intelligent transportation systems
- Applications in traffic optimization, mobility prediction, V2X communication, intelligent routing, and real-time decision-making
- Research opportunities and challenges: hardware limitations, scalability, algorithm maturity, and integration with existing transportation infrastructure
