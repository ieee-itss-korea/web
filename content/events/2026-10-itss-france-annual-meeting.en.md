**The IEEE ITSS France Chapter invites the ITS community to its 2nd Annual Meeting — in person in Paris or online.**

The IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Society France Chapter is holding its 2nd Annual Meeting, bringing the French ITS community together to share recent chapter activities, exchange ideas, strengthen collaborations, and discuss future initiatives in intelligent mobility. Researchers, industry professionals, students, and members of the wider ITS community are welcome.

## Event Details

| | |
|---|---|
| **Date** | Friday, October 2, 2026 |
| **Time** | 09:30–14:00 CEST / 16:30–21:00 KST |
| **Format** | Hybrid (in person and online) |
| **Venue** | Centre International de Conférences Sorbonne Université (CICSU), Room 116, Campus Pierre et Marie Curie, 4 place Jussieu, 75005 Paris |
| **Organizer** | IEEE ITSS France Chapter |

## Program Highlights

- **Welcome remarks** — Chapter introduction (Mostafa Ameli), remarks by the IEEE ITSS Governor (Arnaud de La Fortelle) and the IEEE France Section President (Florence Sèdes)
- **Chapter annual report** — objectives, activities, industry relations, and joint events; Women in Engineering and Transportation; Chapter Awards; regular meetings and call for contributions
- **IEEE ITSS Best PhD Presentation Prize** — presentations by the selected finalists
- **Brainstorming & Q&A** — open discussion on the Chapter's future activities
- **Award announcement and closing remarks**
- **Networking lunch**

*The agenda is tentative and may be subject to minor modifications.*
