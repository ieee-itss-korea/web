## Abstract

The lecture explores an approach to making Vision-Language-Action (VLA) models more efficient and scalable for automated driving, introducing novel architectures and annotation methods to address key challenges in real-world deployment.

## Topics Covered

- An approach to making Vision-Language-Action models fast, lightweight, and scalable
- The FIVE-VLA architecture and its recursive memory mechanism for temporal context management
- LADA and its approach to reducing language annotation dependency to below 5%
- Key deployment challenges, including edge-device inference, closed-loop evaluation, and language collapse
- Bridging the gap between AI research and real-world ADAS deployment
