**ITSC 2028** is the IEEE International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSC), the flagship annual conference of the IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Society (ITSS). It will be held in Tokyo, Japan, on September 12–15, 2028.

Tokyo is a short trip from Korea, which makes ITSC 2028 a good opportunity for members to present their research.

The conference website is not open yet. Important dates will be added to this page once they are announced.

For updates, see the [ITSC 2028 website](https://ieee-itsc.org/2028/) and the [ITSC conference series page](https://ieee-itsc.org/).
