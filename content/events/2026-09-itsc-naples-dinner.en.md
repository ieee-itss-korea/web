**IEEE ITSS Korea Chapter Networking Dinner @ ITSC 2026 — Naples**

Held alongside the **IEEE International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSC 2026)** in Naples, this networking dinner brings together Korean researchers, engineers, and graduate students attending the conference, and welcomes friends of the Korea Chapter from the wider ITSS community. Over a relaxed dinner at a Neapolitan restaurant, connect with peers, exchange ideas on ITSC 2026 papers, and build lasting links across the intelligent transportation systems community.

> **Please register by Friday, September 12, 2026.** Seats are limited — please register via the IEEE vTools event page so we can confirm the final headcount with the restaurant.
