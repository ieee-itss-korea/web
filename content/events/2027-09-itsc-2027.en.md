**ITSC 2027** is the 30th IEEE International Conference on Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITSC), the flagship annual conference of the IEEE Intelligent Transportation Systems Society (ITSS). Researchers, engineers, and students from industry, academia, and government gather to present and discuss the latest advances in intelligent transportation systems.

The conference will feature plenary talks, technical sessions, poster sessions, exhibitions, industry panels, and demonstrations.
