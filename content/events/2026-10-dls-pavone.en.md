## Abstract

The talk explores how reasoning models enable robots and autonomous vehicles to perceive, understand, and act in the real world through contextual and causal reasoning, and addresses the challenges of safety and real-world deployment.

## Topics Covered

- Reasoning-centric AI for Physical AI systems
- Chain-of-thought reasoning in autonomous driving
- Model design and training strategies for causal reasoning
- Tools and datasets for development and evaluation
- Safety, reliability, and challenges in real-world deployment
